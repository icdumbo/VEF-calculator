Privacy Policy

Vessel VEF Calculator — Android application, including the BASIC edition

Effective date and last updated: October 9, 2026

About this policy

Vessel VEF Calculator is a free, advertising-supported Android application maintained by the developer using the GitHub account icdumbo. Its Android package is app.vessel.vef.basic. This policy explains the calculator's local data handling and the separate data processing performed by Google AdMob, which supplies the banner advertisement.

Calculator information stays on your device

Vessel details, ports or terminals, cargo descriptions, dates, voyage quantities and VEF results are processed locally for your calculations. The calculator does not upload these entries or results to the developer or send them to AdMob as advertising parameters. There is no developer-operated server receiving calculator information.

The current Android BASIC edition has no account registration, cloud synchronization, saved-project database or calculation history. Inputs remain in the running session and are discarded when cleared or when the session ends. The developer does not request your name, email address, payment information, contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location through the calculator.

Google AdMob advertising

The application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display one adaptive banner. Advertising involves network communication with Google. Google's SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. This processing is separate from the local VEF calculation inputs.

  • IP address: Google receives the device's IP address, which can be used to infer an approximate location.
  • Interactions: information about app launches and interactions, such as taps and advertisement views.
  • Diagnostics: performance information about the application and SDK, such as launch timing, hangs and energy usage.
  • Identifiers: the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device. Availability depends on device settings and SDK behavior.

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for details of Google's processing, sharing and privacy controls.

The developer does not maintain a separate database of advertising identifiers or sell calculator entries. AdMob may provide the developer with advertising reports, such as impressions, clicks and revenue. The app does not separately integrate a developer analytics service beyond the advertising SDK.

Your choices

You can use the advertising privacy controls available in Android settings to reset or delete your advertising ID, and use Google's account and advertising controls where applicable. The available options and their labels vary by Android version and device. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising or all SDK data processing.

Calculator functions work without an internet connection; advertisements may then be unavailable. You can clear the current calculation within the app or end the session. Uninstalling removes the app from your device, but does not by itself delete information already processed by Google.

Retention, deletion and privacy inquiries

The developer retains no server-side copy of calculator entries or results. Google determines retention and deletion of information it processes under its policies; this policy does not promise a particular retention period or deletion of Google-held information. Google's privacy resources explain the controls and request mechanisms available for that information.

If you voluntarily submit a support or privacy inquiry, the information you include is used to respond and resolve the inquiry and may remain in GitHub's issue history. Use the contact mechanism below for questions about access, correction or deletion, and avoid posting personal or confidential information publicly.

Children

The application is a maritime calculation tool intended for professional use and is not designed specifically for children. It does not request age information or create user profiles. If you have a concern about a child's information in connection with the app, contact the developer through the mechanism below.

This policy website and external links

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking forms added by the developer. GitHub may process technical information about visits, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External websites and advertised destinations have their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Vessel VEF Calculator GitHub issue form. A GitHub account may be required. Issues are public: do not include passwords, advertising IDs, vessel records or other personal or confidential data. You can start with a general question about how to handle a private request.
